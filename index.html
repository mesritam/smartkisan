Soil health

Soil health information

Healthy soil improves water retention, nutrient uptake, and long-term crop yield.

Nitrogen Leaf and stem growth

Low nitrogen often causes pale leaves. Add compost, green manure, or a balanced fertilizer after testing.

Phosphorus Root development

Important for early growth, flowering, and seed formation. Works best near neutral pH.

Potassium Stress resistance

Helps crops handle drought, disease pressure, and fruit quality.

pH range 6.0 to 7.5 ideal

Soil outside this range can block nutrient absorption even when nutrients are present.
